The
AGRICET
(Agriculture Common Entrance Test) is a state-level entrance examination conducted by
Acharya N.G. Ranga Agricultural University (ANGRAU)
. AGRICET is held for admission, through lateral entry, to the B.Sc (Hons.) Agriculture programme for candidates who have completed a relevant diploma in agriculture or allied fields. Conducted in computer-based (online) mode, the exam assesses candidates on the syllabus prescribed for diploma courses in agriculture, seed technology and organic farming. It is taken by diploma holders aspiring to continue into a degree programme in agriculture. This page covers complete AGRICET details – eligibility, exam pattern, important dates, application process and preparation tips.

About AGRICET
AGRICET is the state-level entrance exam conducted by ANGRAU for lateral-entry admission to the B.Sc (Hons.) Agriculture programme for eligible diploma holders.
What is AGRICET?
State-level agriculture lateral-entry entrance exam.
Conducted by ANGRAU in online (computer-based) mode.
For diploma holders seeking B.Sc (Hons.) Agriculture admission.
Based on the diploma agriculture syllabus.

How to Apply for AGRICET

Register to create login credentials.
Fill the application form.
Upload photograph, signature and documents.
Pay the fee online.
Submit and print the confirmation.

Practise previous papers and mock tests.
